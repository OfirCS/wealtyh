# Free and paid

**The line sits where money starts leaving: a bank login costs real money every month, so it's paid. Everything else is free for good.**

| | Free | Family, C$7.99/month or C$69/year |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gmail, Apple Wallet, statements | Yes | Yes |
| Budget, streak, deadlines, renewals | Yes | Yes |
| Bank and card logins | No | Up to 5 |
| Inboxes | 1 | Up to 4, so a partner counts too |
| Ask your money | 5 a day | 50 a day |

Competitors, in US dollars: YNAB $109/year, Monarch $99.99/year, Copilot $95/year, Simplifi about $48 to $84/year. None has a free plan. Rocket Money does, and charges $7 to $14/month above it. Canadian prices weren't confirmed.

Costs per family per month: AI is cents after a first scan of about $0.81. Plaid rates are only shown after applying. Flinks' entry plan works out to $2.50 a connection. Card fees are about 3% plus 30 cents. Check Plaid's real rate before going public.

Billing stays off until Stripe keys exist, and until then everyone gets Family.

**For the founder to decide:** "If it finds you less than it costs in a year, next year's free." Strong differentiator, but found money can be gamed, so count only what the user confirms. Not on the landing page until approved.
