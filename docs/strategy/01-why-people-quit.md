# Why people quit budget apps

Published retention numbers disagree wildly (day 30 from 4% to 38%), so none are used here. The reasons repeat across sources.

| Why people leave | What Pathfinder does |
| --- | --- |
| Typing things in | Nothing is typed. Gmail, the bank and Wallet fill it |
| Seeing the number feels bad | A streak rewards looking. Overspending can't break it |
| Bank links break | "Sign in again" is one tap, and Gmail, Wallet and statements keep the budget alive meanwhile |
| Nothing comes back for the effort | Deadlines a bank can't see: returns, trials, refunds due. Money found is counted |
| It reports the past | Tomorrow's charges and deadlines lead, not last month's chart |
| Paying before seeing if it works | Free plan works fully without a bank login |

Rule of thumb: every screen must either save a tap or find money.

Sources: [Why people quit in 30 days](https://spendtrak.app/blog/why-people-quit-budgeting-apps), [Why budgeting apps fail](https://www.financialfitnesspassport.com/learn/why-budgeting-apps-fail-most-people), [Ranked by 1-3 star reviews](https://unstar.app/blog/ynab-rocket-money-monarch-everydollar-copilot-budget-apps-ranked-2026). These are blogs, not studies, so read them for themes, not figures.
